Brand identity · v2.0

A shield that holds a claim document with a check: every claim is protected and verified before it reaches the payer. Navy and white, flat and calm, for people who read claims all day.

Shield (navy, Deep Navy rim): protection of every claim. Document (white, folded corner): the claim itself. Check: the pre-validation. Use the navy mark on light surfaces and the white mark on navy. Do not recolour, stretch or add shadows; below 120 px wide, use the mark alone.
